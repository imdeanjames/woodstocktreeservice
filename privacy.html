Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and handled when you use woodstocktreeservice.com or telephone the enquiry number displayed on the website.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. About This Website

Woodstocktreeservice.com operates as an independent tree removal enquiry and referral website. It is not operated by or affiliated with the business that previously used this domain. The site helps homeowners enquire about potential independent service providers and is not itself a tree removal contractor.

2. Information That May Be Collected

When you call, telecommunications systems may collect your caller number, date and time, call duration, routing events and similar operational information. If you leave a voicemail, we may collect your name, callback number, town or ZIP code, details of the tree removal job and your recorded voice message. Website hosting may record IP addresses, browser type and access logs.

3. Purposes of Processing

Information may be used to receive and review tree removal enquiries, understand what services you are seeking, arrange relevant follow-up or referrals where available, maintain call records, identify abuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Referral Providers and Compensation

Where appropriate, enquiry details may be disclosed to independent third-party providers to help respond to your requested service. This site may receive referral compensation for some qualifying enquiries. Providers are independent and responsible for their own services, prices, licensing, insurance and privacy handling. A call does not authorise unrelated marketing communications.

5. Calls, Voicemail and Recording

Calls may be handled by third-party telecommunications and call-tracking systems. In the initial intended setup, callers hear an automated greeting and may elect to continue to voicemail. Voicemail recording takes place after appropriate notice. Voicemail messages may be reviewed, used to respond to your enquiry and shared with a relevant provider where appropriate. Live calls might be routed to independent providers in future. Where recording, monitoring or live transfer is introduced, the service will provide any notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law. Do not leave payment card numbers or other sensitive details in voicemail.

6. Website Hosting and Cookies

This static website does not currently contain an enquiry form. Normal hosting infrastructure may retain logs. If additional analytics, marketing cookies or advertising technology is added, relevant disclosures and consent arrangements will be updated as required.

7. Retention and Security

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably needed for enquiry handling, recordkeeping, operational security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. No specific fixed retention period is promised. Reasonable safeguards may be applied, but no internet or telecommunications system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may be entitled to access, correct or request deletion of information held by this website operator. Contact dean AT djfpublishing DOT com, mentioning woodstocktreeservice.com. Independent service providers may need to respond separately concerning information they hold.

9. Third-Party Services

Hosting and telephone infrastructure providers may process information to provide technical services. Independent contractors or other referrals may have their own privacy policies. We do not control all third-party practices.

10. Changes

This policy may be revised as website features, referral arrangements, applicable legal requirements and call-routing systems develop. The version posted here is the current policy.
